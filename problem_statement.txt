Cardiologist Clinical Patient Review & Decision-Support Agent

Problem Statement

Cardiologists need to review information from multiple sources when assessing a patient's cardiovascular condition. Relevant information such as patient history, medications, allergies, laboratory results, clinical guidelines, and drug information may be stored in different systems.
This fragmented workflow can make it difficult to quickly obtain a complete, evidence-backed view of a patient's cardiovascular status.
The proposed solution is a Cardiologist Clinical Patient Review & Decision-Support Agent that uses an AI agent to retrieve and synthesize information from three specialized tools:
Primary user: Cardiologist

Example workflow

A cardiologist is reviewing a patient with a history of hypertension and heart disease.
Instead of manually checking several sources, the cardiologist can ask:

"Review patient P1005's cardiovascular history, current medications, recent laboratory results, and relevant cardiology guidelines. Highlight information that may require my attention."

The agent determines which tools are required.
